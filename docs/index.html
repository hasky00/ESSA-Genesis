Mathematics of SELF

Identity and state

Conceptual representation: \(\mathcal E_t=(I,E,S_t,B_t,M_t)\). Identity is preserved by design: \(I_{t+1}=I_t\).

Bayesian learning

For each substrate and action, \(\theta_{s,a}\sim\mathrm{Beta}(\alpha,\beta)\), with predicted success \(\hat p=\frac{\alpha}{\alpha+\beta}\). After observing \(y\in\{0,1\}\), update \((\alpha,\beta)\leftarrow(\alpha+y,\beta+1-y)\).

Observed C/action 0: \(614/877\approx70.01\%\).

Prediction error and reconsideration

Squared prediction error: \(e_t=(\hat p_t-y_t)^2\). The one-sided detector uses \(D_t=\max(0,D_{t-1}+\hat p_t-y_t-0.1)\). After a 50-cycle warm-up, an alarm occurs at \(D_t\ge8\). Both C belief pairs reset to (1,1), then the current observation is incorporated. The detector and warm-up counter reset; identity and history persist.
